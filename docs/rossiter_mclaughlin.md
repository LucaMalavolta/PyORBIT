# Rossiter–McLaughlin models

```{warning}
 This page is a stub generated by Codex. The content has **not** been verified yet; important information (references, links to external packages) may be missing, and mistakes may be present
```

These models describe the spectroscopic signal produced when a transiting
body occults different parts of a rotating stellar surface. All variants share
the planet geometry, stellar rotation and limb-darkening common objects, but
they target different observables: a classical radial-velocity anomaly, local
velocities from reloaded RM analysis, or two-dimensional CCF residuals.
